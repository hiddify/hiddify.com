    The story of Hiddify

    Internet Freedom Solution

    In Iran, internet access is heavily censored, even more so after Mahsa Amini's death and the protests that followed. Since then, everyday people have faced restrictions in reaching the open internet.

    The Hiddify project gives people simple and affordable ways to reach the unrestricted internet.

    Hidden+ Simple= Hiddify
