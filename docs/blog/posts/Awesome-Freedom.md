# Awesome Freedom: one list of tools to get past the filter

<!-- made by .cache/blog/history_gen.py -->

!!! abstract "In 30 seconds"
    Two weeks after the internet was shut down, on 6 October 2022, we published **Awesome Freedom**: one list of
    the tools that still worked (Telegram proxies, Shadowsocks, Outline, Clash apps) and of **sites that sell
    servers**, so anyone with a little technical knowledge could make their own VPN. It has more than **1,200
    stars** on GitHub.

<!-- more -->

## What was new

- **A review of filtering methods** and of the tools that still worked in Iran.
- **Telegram proxies**, Gost, Shadowsocks, Outline, SoftEther and Clash apps in one place.
- **A list of VPS providers**, with the ways to pay for them.
- **Community pull requests** kept it up to date until November 2024.
- It was the seed of the one-click server installs that became Hiddify Manager.

## Today

Today these tools live inside the Hiddify app and Hiddify Manager. Start with the app, or run your own server.
