# Hiddify turns three

!!! abstract "In 30 seconds"
    Hiddify turned three on 23 September 2025. In its third year the Hiddify app reached the **App Store**, the
    Hiddify core got **extensions**, and Hiddify Manager became lighter and faster with **Docker installs** and
    **version 11**. This post looks back at year three, from the [second anniversary](2nd-anniversary.md) to today.

<!-- more -->

## Year three in a few numbers

- **Hiddify Manager**: 28 stable releases, from 10.70 to **11.0**.
  ([GitHub](https://github.com/hiddify/Hiddify-Manager/releases))
- **Hiddify app**: version **2.5**, and close to 500 commits for the next generation of the app.
  ([GitHub](https://github.com/hiddify/hiddify-app))
- **Hiddify core**: from version **3.0** to **3.2**.
  ([GitHub](https://github.com/hiddify/hiddify-core/releases))

*(23 September 2024 to 23 September 2025, from the GitHub releases and commits.)*

## Hiddify app

- **On the App Store** (October 2024): [the Hiddify app was approved](App_published_on_the_AppStore.md) and now
  installs on iPhone and iPad from Apple's official store too, next to Android, Windows, Linux and Mac.
- **Version 2.5** (September 2024): the last version of the 2.x line. After it, work began on the next generation
  of the app, which came out later as [Hiddify app 4](Hiddify-app-v4-is-released.md).

## Hiddify core

- [**Extensions**](Hiddify-Extensions.md) (September 2024): with core 3, developers can add new features and pages to
  the Hiddify app. The first extension was an IP scanner.

## Hiddify Manager

- [**Version 10.80**](Hiddify-Manager-v10-80-is-released.md) (December 2024): Docker became a real way to install,
  WireGuard and Shadowsocks were added to the v2rayNG links, and installing got faster.
- [**Version 11**](Hiddify-Manager-v11-is-released.md) (July 2025): XHTTP with separate upload and download domains,
  QUIC, a rebuilt proxy system, and up to half the memory use.

## Thank you

Hiddify is made by a small team and many volunteers: people who translate the app, try the test versions, report
problems and answer questions in the groups. Thank you all. Our goal is the same as on the first day: the open
internet is a right for everyone.

How you can help:

- Star the projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/hiddify) and rate the app in the stores.
- Tell a friend and share our guides.
- Report problems from inside the app or on GitHub.
- If you can, [support us](/donation-and-support/).
