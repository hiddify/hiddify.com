# Hiddify turns four

!!! abstract "In 30 seconds"
    Hiddify started on 23 September 2022, when the internet in Iran was shut down. Four years later, the
    Hiddify app is at version 5 and Hiddify Manager at version 14, both still free, open source and
    without ads. This post looks back at the last two years, since our
    [second anniversary](2nd-anniversary.md).

<!-- more -->

## Two years in numbers

- **Hiddify app**: from version 2.5 to **5**. More than **34,000** stars on
  [GitHub](https://github.com/hiddify/hiddify-app).
- **Hiddify Manager**: from version 10 to **14**, four big releases. More than **9,600** stars on
  [GitHub](https://github.com/hiddify/Hiddify-Manager).
- One shared engine, [hiddify-core](https://github.com/hiddify/hiddify-core), under both.

*(Star counts checked on 16 November 2026.)*

## What the Hiddify app learned

- **No server? No problem.** [Free profiles](/app/Free-profiles-in-Hiddify-app/) with WARP and Psiphon
  connect without a subscription link (version 4).
- **Chain** (version 5): [Extra security and Proxy unblocker](/app/Chain-Extra-security-and-Proxy-unblocker/)
  put WARP or Psiphon in front of or behind your own proxy with one choice.
- **One connection for the whole home**: [share your connection](/app/Share-your-connection/) with a
  computer or a TV, and [send a profile to Android TV](/app/Hiddify-on-Android-TV/) from your phone.
- **Routing that thinks for you**: the app picks which apps go through the proxy
  ([Routing](/app/Routing-in-Hiddify-app/)).

## What Hiddify Manager learned

- [**Version 11**](Hiddify-Manager-v11-is-released.md) (July 2025): XHTTP and separate upload and download domains.
- [**Version 12**](Hiddify-Manager-v12-is-released.md) (February 2026): Mieru, Naive, HTTP/2, ECH and a Telegram proxy that counts traffic.
- [**Version 13**](Hiddify-Manager-v13-is-released.md) (September 2026): many servers from one panel, DNS tunnels for heavy filtering, a new dashboard.
- [**Version 14**](Hiddify-Manager-v14-is-released.md) (October 2026): a new, simpler admin panel with quick setup, Hysteria, TUIC and OpenVPN.

## A new home for the guides

This website was rewritten too: the [app](/app/) and [manager](/manager/) guides have new screenshots,

## Thank you

Hiddify is made by a small team and many volunteers: people who translate the app, test beta versions,
report bugs and answer questions in the groups. Thank you. Our goal has not changed: free internet is
everyone's right.

How you can help:

- Give the projects a star on [GitHub](https://github.com/hiddify) and rate the app in the stores.
- Tell a friend, and share our guides.
- Report problems from the app or on GitHub.
- [Support us](/donation-and-support/) if you can.
