# Hiddify Next 0.11: VPN, system proxy or proxy only

<!-- made by .cache/blog/history_gen.py -->

!!! abstract "In 30 seconds"
    Hiddify Next **0.11** came out on 19 November 2023. You can now choose **how the app connects**: as a VPN for
    the whole device, as the **system proxy**, or as a **proxy only** for apps you set yourself. It also works
    well on every screen size.

<!-- more -->

## What was new

- **Service modes:** VPN (TUN), System proxy, or Proxy only.
- **Share** a config as JSON, or a subscription as a QR code.
- **A new tray menu** on desktop, and **connect on start**.
- **winget:** install and update on Windows with `winget`.
- **Fits every screen**, from phones to big monitors.
- **Turkish language**, and the sing-box 1.7 core.

## Today

The Hiddify app is at version 5 now, with free profiles, Chain and connection sharing. Download the latest version.
